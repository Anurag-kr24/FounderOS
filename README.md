# FounderOS

### Your AI Co-Founder

FounderOS is an AI-powered decision engine designed to help builders and founders evaluate startup ideas, analyze markets, identify competitors, and generate execution plans through collaborative AI agents.

## Vision

Transforming ideas into reality in minutes.

FounderOS aims to become an AI Chief of Staff for founders — helping builders understand markets, competitors, opportunities, and execution before spending months building.

## Philosophy

FounderOS doesn't try to motivate founders.

It tries to tell them the truth.

Its goal is to help builders see the reality of their ideas before spending months building them.

## Core Features

- Reality Engine™
- Startup Opportunity Scoring
- Competitor Analysis
- Stakeholder Mapping
- Suggested Pivots
- Technical Architecture Generation
- Product Roadmaps
- PRD Generation
